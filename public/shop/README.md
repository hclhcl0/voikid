# Little Market artwork

`storefront.webp` is an original environment generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then converted to WebP with sharp. No third-party artwork was used as a generation reference. Characters use the project's original flower, star and rainbow sprites. Product icons use the existing Fluent Emoji/Twemoji renderer and licenses.

`shopkeeper.webp` is an original transparent chibi shopkeeper generated with the built-in imagegen tool and optimized to WebP with sharp. The character appears on the welcome, play and results screens.

Prompt: A professional mobile casual game environment, landscape 1536x1024, adorable pastel fantasy village store viewed straight on. Premium painted 2.5D illustration, warm lighting, mint/peach striped awning, cream wall, teal window, shelves and baskets at edges. Clean mint space in upper center for real character/UI overlays, honey wooden counter in bottom third, flowering plants and fairy lights in corners. No people, words, logos, buttons or watermarks.

Shopkeeper prompt: Full-body transparent 2D game cutout, one cheerful chibi girl shopkeeper with warm medium skin, short dark chestnut bob, coral flower hair clip and hazel eyes. Cream puff-sleeve blouse, sage mint apron with peach trim and stitched flower, brown boots. Waving hello and holding a small woven basket. Premium polished pastel 2.5D game art, readable at 120 px, no scene, text, logo, other characters, weapon or wings.

The real UI is rendered separately and is keyboard accessible. This game uses 500 curated everyday products across 17 shelves, including Vietnamese meals, market groceries, household supplies, school items, clothing and festival goods. The original 56 product IDs remain stable. Exact emoji are used where available; other products show a neutral shopping-bag icon alongside the Vietnamese label, avoiding misleading pictures. Shelf selection is paginated. Each session has eight customers: up to six new items and later review items, prioritizing misses or hints. Completed profile sessions rotate through the shelf before repeating its initial items; rewards are persisted via profile progress once per session.

Shop speech requests only the configured ElevenLabs media endpoint. It never falls back to a system voice. Customers rotate among three natural request sentences, and a repeated item uses a different sentence. In normal mode, the sentence appears with an underlined slot and the product word flies into that slot; challenge mode hides the sentence until the learner requests a hint. Audio reads only the product name, including automatic reading at each new customer and replay from the result. Authenticated students can generate only the 500 curated product names, under the media route's rate limit; generated MP3s are cached for later plays. Guests can play cached MP3s for those shop products, but cannot generate audio or access other saved media. Admins can prepare product MP3s in Admin → Media. If a word has no cached audio, the English request is displayed so the game remains playable.
